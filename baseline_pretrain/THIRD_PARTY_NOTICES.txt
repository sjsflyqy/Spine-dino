1. Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates: DINO, DINOv2 and MAE.
   https://github.com/facebookresearch/dino
   https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2
   https://github.com/facebookresearch/mae
   Transformer blocks, positional interpolation, weight-normalized DINO heads,
   self-distillation, Sinkhorn normalization, block masks, KoLeo and MAE masking
   and reconstruction equations are adapted from these projects and the local
   references below. Original Meta copyright notices are retained here.

2. Joshua Scheuplein et al., DINO Adapted to X-Ray (DAX).
   https://github.com/JoshuaScheuplein/DAX
   Local source: reference_code/baseline/DAX/DAX-main/
   ViT-S architecture, antialiased positional interpolation and multi-crop
   augmentation settings. The raw 16-bit TIFF preprocessing is deliberately
   not applied to the repository's already rendered spine PNG/JPEG images.

3. CheXFound contributors, CheXFound.
   Local source: reference_code/baseline/CheXFound/CheXFound-main/
   ViT-L/16 with registers, SwiGLU, LayerScale, high-drop-rate subset stochastic
   depth, DINO/iBOT/KoLeo objectives and public checkpoint layouts.

4. Junfei Xiao, Yutong Bai, Alan Yuille, Zongwei Zhou, Medical MAE.
   https://github.com/lambert-x/medical_mae
   Local source: reference_code/baseline/medical_MAE/medical_mae-main/
   ViT-B MAE architecture, decoder, random masking and masked pixel loss.

Changes in this package: explicit namespaced modules; PyTorch SDPA instead of
timm/xFormers imports; read-only spine index; strict public initialization;
optimizer-update budgets; resumable deterministic sample streams; mixed
precision, DDP/FSDP and activation checkpointing; independent artifacts and
safe tensor-checkpoint loading. See README.md for scientific protocol changes.
